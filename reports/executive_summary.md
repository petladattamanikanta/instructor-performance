# Executive Summary: Instructor Performance & Course Quality Evaluation
**Target Audience:** EduPro Executive Board, Management, and Public Stakeholders  
**Author:** Senior Data Analyst & Data Scientist  
**Date:** June 3, 2026  

---

### Executive Overview
This summary outlines the findings from a comprehensive analysis of the EduPro platform (3,000 students, 60 instructors, 60 courses, 10,000 enrollments in 2025). The goal is to provide data-driven strategic direction to improve teaching quality, content engagement, and mitigate severe human capital risks.

---

### Core KPI Dashboard Results
- **Average Teacher Rating:** **3.13 / 5.00** *(Moderate, needs training interventions)*
- **Average Course Rating:** **3.10 / 5.00** *(Underperforming, indicates content quality gaps)*
- **Rating Consistency Index:** **43.39 / 100** *(High rating volatility; courses lack standardized student satisfaction)*
- **Experience Impact Score:** **+0.598** *(Strong positive correlation; instructor rating increases with experience)*
- **Enrollment Influence Ratio:** **1.05** *(Higher course ratings drive only a 5% increase in enrollment, showing enrollment is decoupled from course quality)*
- **Instructor Reliability Index:** **27.14 / 100** *(Very low; indicates platform quality is highly volatile and dependent on a few people)*
- **Course Excellence Index:** **26.67%** *(Only 16 out of 60 courses are rated above 4.0)*
- **Best-Performing Domain:** **Marketing** *(Average Course Rating of 3.65)*

---

### Key Findings
* **The Two-Teacher Monopoly:** Two instructors—Yolanda Levine (Machine Learning) and Kimberly Miller (Cybersecurity)—account for **60.86% (6,086)** of all transactions on the platform. 
* **Widespread Underperformance:** The largest segment of instructors is **At-Risk Instructors** ($n=23$, 38.3%), who are novice teachers (mean experience of 2.65 years) and average a poor rating of **2.16**.
* **Decoupled Quality Dimensions:** Statistical correlation confirms that teacher ratings and course content ratings are completely unrelated ($r = 0.0003, p = 0.998$). Stellar delivery does not make up for weak course material, and vice-versa.
* **Experience is Key:** Years of experience is a major driver of teacher ratings ($r = 0.598$), indicating that pedagogical skills are developed over time on the platform.

---

### Major Risks
1. **Human Capital Dependency Risk:** If Yolanda Levine or Kimberly Miller leaves, the platform could lose up to 60% of its enrollments overnight.
2. **Platform Brand Degradation:** With 38.3% of the instructor pool being highly rated "At-Risk" and average course ratings hovering at 3.10/5.00, student churn could increase due to poor experience.
3. **Severe Resource Underutilization:** 35 out of 60 instructors (Growing and Average segments) teach fewer than 20% of the platform’s students combined, despite having solid ratings (3.61 - 3.74).

---

### Major Opportunities
1. **Scale Underutilized Talent:** By redistributing courses from the Star Performers to the "Growing" and "Average" tiers, EduPro can increase utilization and de-risk operations.
2. **Standardize High-Yield Curriculums:** Standardizing course content separate from teachers will raise the Course Excellence Index above the current 26.67%.
3. **Structured Mentorship Program:** Leveraging the 2 Star Performers to train the 23 At-Risk instructors can rapidly elevate platform-wide teaching standards.

---

### Strategic Action Plan (Roadmap)

#### Phase 1: Operational Stabilization (Month 1-2)
- **Implement Co-Teaching:** Pair "Growing" and "Average" instructors with Yolanda Levine and Kimberly Miller on highly enrolled courses to distribute the student load and build instructor visibility.
- **Urgent Course Review:** Audit the bottom 10% of courses with ratings below 2.0. Redesign slides, code labs, and assessments.

#### Phase 2: Talent Development (Month 3-6)
- **Establish Mentorship Circles:** Task Yolanda and Kimberly with leading pedagogical workshops for the 23 "At-Risk" instructors.
- **Restructured Compensation:** Move from experience-only pay scales to a performance-based bonus structure that rewards instructors who maintain high ratings and consistency.

#### Phase 3: Brand Re-positioning (Month 6+)
- **Market High-Performing Domains:** Invest marketing dollars into the high-rated domains (Marketing, Digital Marketing, Data Science) to balance the platform's profile.
- **Hiring Re-alignment:** Modify the hiring process to prioritize candidates with 5+ years of pedagogical experience, as experience is the single strongest predictor of instructor rating on the platform.
